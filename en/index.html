Hi, I am Moritz Vollmer, a Digital Strategist based in Cologne. I connect design, web development and organic visibility so that websites are easy to understand, work reliably and make their content discoverable.

Located: Cologne
Current: Hottgenroth Software AG

About

My perspective connects design, technology and visibility: from visual communication and web development to how people find useful content. I currently work as an SEO & GEO Manager at Hottgenroth Software AG.

Professional background

My design background started at line communication. At Friendventure and Alpha Alpaka UX, I worked in frontend and WordPress development. Today I connect that experience with SEO, GEO and search and usage data. I trained as a Media Designer Digital & Print and am an IHK-certified trainer.
